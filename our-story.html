Our Story

Friends first.
Then, apparently, forever.

The short version: Carnegie Mellon, a lot of shared meals, and two people who — against the odds of graduate school — actually showed up to class.

Chapter One

Carnegie Mellon

Caitlin and Paul met in graduate school, where they started out as friends and quickly discovered they had a few important things in common: a love of food, a love of travel, and an unusually strong commitment to actually attending class.

In a program where "I'll just watch the recording later" was a lifestyle for most people, showing up consistently turned out to be a surprisingly good filter for finding your person.

Chapter Two

Friendship, slowly rerouted

Somewhere between group projects, late dinners, and trips together, friendship turned into something more. Neither of them can point to the exact moment — only that it stopped making sense to plan a trip, or a meal, or much of anything, without the other one.

What started as "let's split a plate of tacos to compare notes" became a shared list of restaurants, then a shared apartment, then a shared life.

Chapter Three

The good idea

Eventually, they decided that spending the rest of their lives together sounded like a pretty good idea — the kind of decision that felt obvious once they finally said it out loud.

Now they're heading to Mexico City to celebrate with the people they love most: two cities' worth of friends and family, one very good excuse for a trip, and a wedding at Casa Xipe to make it official.

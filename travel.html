For a lot of you, this will be a first trip to Mexico City. Here's everything you need to know, from passports to pesos.

First Time in Mexico City?

A huge, modern, sophisticated city.

CDMX is one of the largest cities in the world, and one of the most exciting — world-class restaurants, museums that rival any in Europe, striking modern and colonial architecture, and neighborhoods that each feel like their own small city. If this is your first visit, come a little early if you can. There's a lot to love.
